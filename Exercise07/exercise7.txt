The Mandelbrot program was parallelised using OpenMP by dividing the outer loop among multiple threads. A reduction was used for numoutside to safely combine the values calculated by each thread. The program was tested using 1, 2, 3, and 4 threads, and the calculated area remained identical in every case. The execution time decreased as more threads were used, with 4 threads giving the best execution time of 3.255 seconds.
